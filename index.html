About Bean & Bloom

Bean & Bloom is a small, family-run café and bakery in Kathmandu serving coffee and baked goods made fresh each morning.

Our story

We started as a weekend stall sharing home-baked bread and Nepali-grown beans with neighbours. Friends kept coming back, so we opened a space where they could sit down.

Our mission

To make everyday moments feel a little better with honest food, carefully made coffee and a welcoming room.

Why choose us

  • Local beans. Roasted in small batches from Nepali farms.
  • Baked daily. Pastries and cakes made every morning.
  • Room to linger. Comfortable seating and free Wi-Fi.
  • Easy booking. Reserve a table in one message.
